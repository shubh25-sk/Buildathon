# User Guide for MSME Exporters

## How to Calculate Export Costs & Compare Routes

1. **Start New Calculation**: Click "Calculate Export Shipment Costs" on the Dashboard.
2. **Step 1 - Product Details**: Select industry category (Textiles, Agri, Engineering, Pharma, etc.), declared invoice value, currency, and quantity.
3. **Step 2 - Weight & Package**: Input total gross weight (kg), volume (CBM), package type (Box, Pallet, Container), and urgency.
4. **Step 3 - Origin**: Pick your nearest Indian origin city/ICD (e.g. Ludhiana, Delhi, Jaipur, Tirupur, Surat).
5. **Step 4 - Destination**: Pick international destination port (e.g. Hamburg, Rotterdam, Dubai, Singapore, New York).
6. **Step 5 - Incoterm**: Choose contract terms (EXW, FOB, CFR, CIF, DAP, DDP). The system automatically highlights seller vs buyer cost responsibilities.
7. **View Results**: Explore itemized cost tables, Export Cost Indicator (ECI) score, interactive Leaflet trade map, and export reports in PDF/CSV format.
