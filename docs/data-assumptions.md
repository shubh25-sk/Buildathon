# Data Assumptions & Benchmark Pricing

## Freight & Handling Benchmarks (Q3 2026 Indicative Rates)
- **Ocean Freight**: ~$45 / CBM baseline for sea routes.
- **Air Express Freight**: $4.80 / kg for priority air cargo; $3.20 / kg for standard air cargo.
- **Inland Transport**: ₹28 / km per ton haulage rate from Indian factories to container ports.
- **Origin THC**: ₹7,500 for sea port CFS stuffing; ₹4,500 for air cargo handling.
- **Customs & CHA**: ₹4,500 per shipping bill.
- **Marine Cargo Insurance**: 0.35% of 110% CIF invoice value.
- **Contingency Buffer**: 2.5% reserve factor.
