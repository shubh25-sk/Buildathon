# Product Requirements Document (PRD)

## 1. Problem Statement
Indian Micro, Small, and Medium Enterprises (MSMEs) contribute over 45% of India's total exports. However, navigating complex logistics costs, Incoterm allocations (EXW, FOB, CIF, DDP), customs formalities (ICEGATE, CHA fees), and multi-modal route comparisons remains opaque and prohibitive.

## 2. Core Vision & Objectives
Provide an accessible, transparent, and step-by-step export cost calculator and trade route visualizer tailored for non-logistics experts.

### Key Capabilities
- **Itemized Cost Engine**: Breakdown of 15+ export cost components (inland transport, terminal handling, documentation, main carriage, fuel surcharges, insurance, destination charges).
- **Incoterm Responsibility Filter**: Explicit seller vs buyer cost allocation according to ICC Incoterms 2020.
- **Export Cost Indicator (ECI)**: A normalized score (0–100) evaluating Cost Efficiency, Transit Speed, Reliability, Complexity, Risk, and Sustainability.
- **Interactive Trade Map**: Leaflet-based visualization of sea, air, and multimodal trade corridors with port popups.
- **Route Alternatives & Comparison**: Side-by-side comparison matrix with MSME recommendation badges.
- **Report Generation**: Client-side PDF and CSV report downloads.
